Theatre intercom · concept v3 Beltpack
136 × 96 mm · 28 mm deep at the centre, 35 at the ends · knobs +13 mm

Curved, low-profile, FreeSpeak-style. The back follows a 250 mm curve so it sits on the hip, and the pack is 28 mm deep at the centre (FreeSpeak II is 38). The 1.3 in OLED and talk keys sit on a 45° face you read looking down: keys 1–2 left, 3–4 right, two knobs on top, Reply on the front. A flat battery pack clips into the back.
