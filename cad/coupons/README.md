# Beltpack case rev B: test coupons

Small pieces cut straight from the rev B model, so they have exactly the clearances of the real parts. Print them in the
material and settings you'll use for the real case, then check each fit below. Tell me what's tight or loose (and by
roughly how much) and I'll adjust the model before you print the full parts. The STLs are in the case's own frame:
rotate them in the slicer to the orientation given.

| Coupon | Files | Print | What to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Keys | top plate section, talk keys 1 and 2 | top plate face-down; keycaps flange-down | Each keycap drops through its hole from underneath and moves freely with no rattle; the flange stops it. The groove and the left status-light hole are on this piece too: try your light-pipe rod in the hole |
| 2 Buttons | front shell section, Back and Function buttons | front section standing on its cut bottom edge; buttons flange-down | Buttons go in from behind the recess and slide freely; flanges stop them |
| 3 Dial | front shell right end, right dial tyre (TPU) and hub | end section standing on its cut bottom face, with supports under the well floor; tyre wide end down; hub on its flat end | Hub pushes into the tyre (snug enough to glue); the hub's stem turns freely in the well floor's hole; the tyre spins in the well without rubbing. If your encoders have arrived, check the shaft goes into the hub's D-bore |
| 4 Pack snap | back shell bottom section, battery pack bottom section | both standing on their cut bottom face | The pack's bottom tooth clicks under the back shell's edge and holds; you can release it with a thumbnail |
| 5 Dock pocket | front shell bottom wall section | inside face up (flat on the bed, no supports) | The dock contact board drops into the pocket flat, and its four pads line up with the windows |
| 6 TPU corner | front and back shell bottom corner, TPU bottom cap corner | shells bottom-down; TPU section flange-down | The TPU section pushes on over the chamfered corner and sits flush. It's only a section, so it won't grip like the full cap: check the fit and thickness, not the hold |

Total is under 40 cm³ of PETG plus a little TPU, about an hour and a half on a Bambu.
